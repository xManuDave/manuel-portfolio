# Texture sources

All maps in this directory are 1K JPG textures from Poly Haven and are published under CC0.

- `wood-table`: [Wood Table](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table)
- `white-plaster-02`: [White Plaster 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02)
- `fabric-pattern-07`: [Fabric Pattern 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07)
- `plank-flooring-02`: [Plank Flooring 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/plank_flooring_02) — diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and AO
- License: [Poly Haven CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

The materials are deliberately tinted and used with restrained normal strength to fit the stylized art direction.
